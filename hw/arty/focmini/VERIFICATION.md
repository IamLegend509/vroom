## Timing issue fixed

The first routed implementation failed its 10ns clock with -22.798ns setup
slack: encoder-difference, scaling and filtering were chained in one cycle.
A first pipeline still failed at a full-width transform multiplier. The final
implementation registers velocity/filter/PI steps, uses explicitly bounded
20x18 transform multipliers with unchanged fractional precision, and replaces
combinational PWM division with exact 20-clock dividers. Numerical regression
still passes. No clock relaxation or multicycle/false-path exception hides a
DUT synchronous calculation.

The final fixture registers every nonclock input and captures every output,
keeping them variable and observable. Its accepted timing includes fixture-to-DUT,
DUT-internal and DUT-to-fixture register paths. External pin paths use diagnostic
zero-delay assumptions and are **not** accepted as board timing. Those external
reports can contain hold violations and missing partition-pin-location warnings.
The +0.220ns setup margin is small; sensor/board integration needs fresh timing
closure. No bitstream is generated by this verification flow.

## Closed-loop evidence and one remaining failure

The test executes the actual 100MHz RTL. PWM high times drive an averaged
three-phase inverter model, followed by a double-precision RK4 dq motor model
at 50us steps. Phase currents and quantized 12-bit encoder feedback return at
10kHz. Targets are +1rad/s, -1rad/s and +0.5rad/s, with a load step at 1.5s.
Each tracking gate uses the last 150ms of its command interval: absolute mean
error <0.15rad/s and RMSE <0.4rad/s. Both fixtures also require no RTL fault,
phase current <1A, speed <5rad/s, and correct STOP/output limits.

| Assumed fixture | Mean errors, three windows (rad/s) | Peak phase current | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nominal | -0.01159, +0.02087, -0.02108 | 0.62745A | Pass all tracking windows |
| Heavy, reversed encoder | +0.07402, **+0.16167**, -0.13550 | 0.50023A | Reversal mean-error gate fails |

Nominal assumptions: R=1ohm, L=1mH, J=0.001kg m², Ke=0.02Vs/rad,
Kt=0.03Nm/A, friction=0.001Nms/rad. Heavy assumptions: R=1.5ohm, L=2mH,
J=0.0015kg m², Ke=0.01Vs/rad, Kt=0.015Nm/A, same friction. The load step is
0.001Nm. These are synthetic test fixtures, not measured 2805 parameters.

The heavier fixture remains within the current/speed bounds and has no RTL
fault; it misses the declared transient tracking gate. The original ESP32
PI gains are retained rather than tuned to invented motor parameters. The
nominal current peak also demonstrates that the 0.5A **reference** bound does
not guarantee a 0.5A instantaneous phase-current ceiling; the fixture's separate
raw phase-trip setting is 1A.
